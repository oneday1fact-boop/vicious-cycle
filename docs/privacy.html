Vicious Cycle — Privacy Policy

Effective: 29 October 2026

Vicious Cycle (惡性循環), package io.github.oneday1factboop.viciouscycle, is an offline interactive story published by the owner of the Vicious Cycle repository.

Data and device storage

The publisher does not receive story progress or reading settings. There is no account or in-app purchase. The app includes Google AdMob advertising and requests Internet and network-state access. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may access an advertising identifier when available.

Story progress, decisions, ending collections, reading settings and language preferences are stored locally in app storage on your device. The publisher cannot access this information. It stays until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app.

Services and audience

Native Android Views and Canvas render bundled story content. Google AdMob may process IP addresses (including approximate location), advertising and device identifiers, diagnostics, and ad impressions or interactions to serve ads, measure performance and prevent fraud. Google may share data for these purposes under its policies. See Google’s privacy policy and Mobile Ads SDK disclosure. The app requests privacy consent where required before requesting live ads. Available ad privacy choices can be reopened from the game menu. You may also manage/reset your advertising ID in Android settings. Ads are optional to story progression; no connection or unavailable ads never blocks play. Google Play and Android may process information under their own terms when you download, update or use your device. This crime story contains mature themes and is not designed for children.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact the repository owner through GitHub Issues. Please do not post private information in public issues. Any changes to this policy will be published on this page.

惡性循環 — 私隱政策

生效日期：2026年10月29日

《惡性循環》是離線互動故事。發行者不會接收故事進度或閱讀設定。程式沒有帳戶或應用程式內購買，包含Google AdMob廣告，並要求網絡及網絡狀態存取權。Google廣告SDK可能存取可用的廣告識別碼。

遊戲進度、選擇、結局收藏、閱讀設定及語言偏好只儲存在此裝置的應用程式本機儲存空間。發行者無法存取。清除程式資料或解除安裝會刪除此資料；本程式已停用Android雲端備份。

故事由原生Android介面及Canvas顯示。Google AdMob可能處理IP位址（包括概略位置）、裝置及廣告識別資料、診斷資料與廣告曝光或互動，以提供廣告、衡量成效及防止詐騙，並可能按其政策分享相關資料。適用地區會在載入正式廣告前顯示同意選項；可用的廣告私隱選項可隨時從遊戲選單重開。你亦可於Android設定管理或重設廣告識別碼。廣告載入失敗或沒有網絡不會阻止故事推進。下載、更新或使用裝置時，Google Play及Android可能按其各自的政策處理資料。本犯罪故事包含成人主題，並非為兒童設計。

私隱問題請透過上述GitHub Issues聯絡儲存庫擁有人，請勿在公開問題中張貼私人資料。政策更改會於本頁公布。
