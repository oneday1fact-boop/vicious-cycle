Vicious Cycle — Privacy Policy

Effective: 29 October 2026

Vicious Cycle (惡性循環), package io.github.oneday1factboop.viciouscycle, is an offline interactive story published by the owner of the Vicious Cycle repository.

Data and device storage

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. It has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking or in-app purchases, and requests no Internet or sensitive device permissions.

Story progress, decisions, ending collections, reading settings and language preferences are stored locally in Android System WebView storage on your device. The publisher cannot access this information. It stays until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app.

Services and audience

Android System WebView renders locally bundled content. The game itself contacts no third-party services. Google Play and Android may process information under their own terms when you download, update or use your device. This crime story contains mature themes and is not designed for children.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact the repository owner through GitHub Issues. Please do not post private information in public issues. Any changes to this policy will be published on this page.

惡性循環 — 私隱政策

生效日期：2026年10月29日

《惡性循環》是離線互動故事。程式不收集、傳送、出售或分享個人資料，沒有帳戶、廣告、分析、追蹤或應用程式內購買，也不要求網絡或敏感裝置權限。

遊戲進度、選擇、結局收藏、閱讀設定及語言偏好只儲存在此裝置的Android System WebView本機儲存空間。發行者無法存取。清除程式資料或解除安裝會刪除此資料；本程式已停用Android雲端備份。

遊戲由Android System WebView顯示本機內容，不連接第三方服務。下載、更新或使用裝置時，Google Play及Android可能按其各自的政策處理資料。本犯罪故事包含成人主題，並非為兒童設計。

私隱問題請透過上述GitHub Issues聯絡儲存庫擁有人，請勿在公開問題中張貼私人資料。政策更改會於本頁公布。
