# Android 1.1.0 / version code 3

The start screen uses the existing cover as a full-screen background with a face-aware crop and overlaid title/start controls. Story illustrations, evidence pictures and the ending gallery retain the complete-frame fit from 1.0.1. Portrait and landscape story layouts remain separate from dialogue.

## Audio and haptics

Settings contain independent switches and levels for background music, button sounds and story haptics. They persist in the existing local preferences. The original 32-second ambient composition and 65 ms click are generated by `scripts/generate-audio.py` using NumPy and ffmpeg; the generated Ogg files are bundled in `res/raw`. They use no third-party recordings or streaming service.

Music follows Android audio focus, pauses when the activity backgrounds and during full-screen ads, and releases its player/focus on destruction. Button sounds use a separate SoundPool level. Native dialog buttons also follow the game's sound switch. Story haptics use short, non-repeating waveforms, respect Android's haptic-feedback setting, tolerate devices without vibration/amplitude support, and are not repeated on a passage rerender. There is a vibration test in settings. No microphone permission is requested.

## Languages

- Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese and English stories are bundled.
- Japanese, Spanish and French menus, settings and introductory screens are hand-authored and bundled.
- The three additional story editions use Google ML Kit on-device translation. The player explicitly accepts a one-time language download. All 808 displayed narrative/art fields (699 unique strings) are prepared before switching languages, then saved atomically for offline reading. Story IDs, flags, destinations, choices, clue IDs, saves and collected endings are never translated.
- Character names are protected and restored using a fixed glossary. If a model drops a protected token, text fragments are translated separately while the names are inserted locally.
- Incomplete, failed or cancelled preparation leaves the prior language and save intact. A content fingerprint invalidates stale caches after story changes; updated hand-authored menus replace cached menu text without another translation.
- Model download, first preparation and translation quality have not been reviewed on a physical device in this environment. These editions are automatic translations, not professional translations. Device testing remains part of the internal test release.

Google Translate powers the automatic editions: https://cloud.google.com/translate. The native app displays the official, unmodified white attribution badge beside automatic results, labels the preparation action “Translate with Google”, and includes the required disclaimer in Privacy policy. The image in `res/drawable-nodpi/google_translate_attribution.png` is `png/white-regular@3x.png` from Google's official attribution ZIP: https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/translate/images/google-translate-attribution.zip. Google retains its trademarks. Usage references: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/language/translation/android and https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/language/translation/translation-terms.

`scripts/export-native.cjs` produces the deterministic data bundle and display-field translation plan. `scripts/generate-simplified.py` uses a locally installed ICU transliterator only when regenerating Simplified Chinese; normal builds use its committed JSON and need no ICU installation.

## Validation and rollout

Native tests cover all ten endings across all six language templates, recovery into later chapters, save/language switching, full translation-pack application without route changes, partial-pack rejection, repeated protected names, bounded haptics, 650 complete-image fit cases and full-screen cover geometry. These tests validate the translation integration with test translations; they do not run Google's language models or simulate physical audio/haptics.

The signed AAB contains every supported ABI. Direct APK outputs are split by ABI; the ARM64 download is for modern ARM phones. Play-installed copies should update through the existing internal testing track to retain the Play signing path and local saves.

Physical review: cover in portrait/landscape; toggle and adjust music/sounds/haptics; background and resume; an ad transition; download and cancel a language; complete preparation, force-close, reopen offline; switch languages at a choice and after an ending; confirm saved progress. No production rollout is performed by the build.
